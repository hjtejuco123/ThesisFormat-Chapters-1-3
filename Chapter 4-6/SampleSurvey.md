
ISO/IEC 25010:2011 is a standard that defines software quality models, including both functional and non-functional requirements. For system evaluation, the non-functional requirements are categorized into several quality characteristics such as **Performance Efficiency**, **Usability**, **Reliability**, **Security**, and **Maintainability**. Below are sample survey questions aligned with these criteria to evaluate a system.

---

### **1. Performance Efficiency**
This refers to the performance of the system relative to the amount of resources used under specific conditions.

**Sample Questions:**
1. How satisfied are you with the system's response time during peak usage hours?
2. Does the system perform consistently well even when multiple users are accessing it simultaneously?
3. How would you rate the system's ability to handle large amounts of data without delays?
4. Does the system consume an acceptable amount of memory or processing power on your device?
5. How often do you experience slow loading times or lag while using the system?

---

### **2. Usability**
This evaluates how easy and pleasant the system is to use for its intended purpose.

**Sample Questions:**
1. How intuitive is the system's interface for new users?
2. How easy is it to navigate through the system’s features and functions?
3. How satisfied are you with the clarity of instructions or help documentation provided by the system?
4. Does the system minimize the need for user training or external assistance?
5. How would you rate the overall user experience when interacting with the system?

---

### **3. Reliability**
This assesses the system’s ability to perform its required functions under stated conditions for a specified period.

**Sample Questions:**
1. How often does the system experience unexpected crashes or downtime?
2. Does the system recover quickly after a failure or interruption?
3. How confident are you in the system’s ability to operate without errors over extended periods?
4. Have you encountered any data loss issues while using the system?
5. How frequently do you encounter bugs or malfunctions in the system?

---

### **4. Security**
This focuses on the system’s ability to protect information and data from unauthorized access or malicious attacks.

**Sample Questions:**
1. How secure do you feel when entering sensitive information into the system?
2. Are you satisfied with the system’s authentication mechanisms (e.g., passwords, biometrics)?
3. Does the system provide adequate protection against unauthorized access or breaches?
4. How effective are the system’s measures for detecting and preventing security threats?
5. How confident are you that your data is encrypted and stored securely within the system?

---

### **5. Maintainability**
This evaluates how easy it is to modify, update, or fix the system when necessary.

**Sample Questions:**
1. How easy is it for administrators to apply updates or patches to the system?
2. Does the system provide clear error messages that assist in troubleshooting issues?
3. How satisfied are you with the availability of technical support for resolving system problems?
4. Does the system allow for customization or configuration to meet changing business needs?
5. How long does it typically take to resolve issues or implement changes in the system?

---

### Tips for Constructing Survey Questions:
1. **Be Clear and Specific**: Avoid vague terms like "good" or "bad." Use measurable attributes (e.g., "response time," "error frequency").
2. **Use Likert Scales**: Provide rating scales (e.g., 1-5) to quantify responses.
3. **Focus on User Experience**: Frame questions from the perspective of the end-user to gather actionable feedback.
4. **Avoid Leading Questions**: Ensure questions are neutral and unbiased.
5. **Test Your Survey**: Pilot-test the survey with a small group to ensure clarity and relevance.
